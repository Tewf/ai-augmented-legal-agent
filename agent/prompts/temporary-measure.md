# Prompt: open a temporary measure

Load the [skill](../SKILL.md) `legal-constraints` first; its rules hold here.

**Input:** one register line: in the state "open"; or "measure proposed" with its proposed date passed; or
"temporary" with its settle-by date passed, for which you propose a renewed or a new measure.

**Do:**

1. Find the least restrictive measure that lets the work go on while the point waits. Four kinds:
   - later, when the event it must hold before has not come;
   - smaller: fewer people, no data, no money;
   - reversibly: a draft, a test page, a closed beta;
   - as written: the measure the text's own words describe, quoted, and flagged to the respondent as "taken as
     written, to confirm"; whether the words are plain is the respondent's call, not yours.
2. State the risk the measure leaves, in facts only: who is exposed and to what. No likelihood and no sanction
   estimate: those are the respondent's.
3. Propose the settle-by date, with its reason; the approver sets it. For a dated event, propose a date before it.
   For an undated event, propose a date and say what it rests on. For an event already past, because the practice is
   running or public, write "past or running" in "when it must hold". The settle-by date then rests on the risk: the sooner the
   harm the line describes could happen, the nearer the date, with the reason written. Only the smaller and the reversible kinds
   apply then, and the order of the register puts the line near the top. Write the proposed date in "settle by" while
   the state is "measure proposed".
4. If the line's issue column is filled, draft a comment on that issue: the measure, its risk and the date. If not,
   draft the issue that tracks it, in the pattern of `jurist-dossier.md`. A person posts or opens it.
5. For an open line, write it in the state "measure proposed" and ask the approver ([register](../register.md));
   nothing is applied until the approver moves it to "temporary". For a temporary line, keep its state, measure and
   date: the renewal is a proposal in your return, beside the line, for the approver.
6. If every measure would cross a reserved act, say so. If a text's words may forbid every measure, quote them and ask
   the respondent whether the measure falls under them. A person decides whether the work waits.

**Return:** the measure, its risk, the proposed settle-by date with its reason, and the comment's or the issue's
draft. Return also the line in the state "measure proposed", or the temporary line unchanged with the renewal beside it. Run the two guards of the skill on what you return.

**Never:** present the measure as the answer; extend a settle-by date. A later date is proposed with its reason, and
a person approves it.
